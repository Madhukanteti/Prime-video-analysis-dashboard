Conclusion

The analysis of Amazon Prime Video users highlights key trends in subscription behavior, engagement, and content preferences. Smartphones are the most preferred device, horror and action genres are the most popular, and a significant number of users opt for annual subscriptions with auto-renewal. This data can help in making informed business decisions for user retention and targeted content recommendations.
